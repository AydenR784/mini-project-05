Featured Location: Tortuga

A lively pirate port

Tortuga is a busy island port where sailors, pirates, and travelers come together. It is known for its crowded taverns and wild nightlife.

Interesting fact: Tortuga has the highest rates of plunderers in all of the Caribbean. But don't let that stop you from visiting!

Try: Explore the harbor, visit a tavern, and look for ships preparing to sail.
